Enlarge (AI): the enlargement becomes a connected photo

Right-click the highlighted photo (or select it and press Shift+F10) and choose Enlarge (AI)…. The enlargement is saved as a new photo next to the original, cataloged and linked to it, so it can be rated, edited, collected and printed like any other.
