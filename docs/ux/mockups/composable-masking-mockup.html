Composable masking — the Shapes list

Proposed change to the floating mask panel (RFC-0025 §3.4 / slice 1b). A mask keeps its Exposure/Contrast/Saturation; a new Shapes section lists what selects the pixels — the base shape first, then each Add / Subtract / Intersect shape in evaluation order. Click through the walkthrough, or use the panel directly (change a combine mode, delete a shape, pick another row, open Add shape).

◔▭✎◐◍
  • The red fill is the combined selection — the same overlay the mask pass already draws, now following the final weight (slice 1a). It is computed here with the engine's fold, so subtract / intersect show exactly what the renderer produces for these shapes.
  • Row order = evaluation order. (A ∪ B) ∖ C differs from A ∪ (B ∖ C); try moving the same shapes by deleting and re-adding. Reordering by drag is not in the mock (open question).
  • One shape is "being edited" at a time (highlighted row). Only that shape's handles are drawn on the canvas (dashed outline here); the others stay quiet so several shapes do not turn the canvas into a wall of handles. Its own controls (Feather, range…) appear below the list.
  • Mask-level adjustments stay on top and apply to the combined selection — shapes carry no adjustments of their own (RFC §3.2).
  • Caps are shown, not hidden. Shapes n / 16 and the brush-layer budget (8, shared with brush/spot masks) sit in the section header; Add shape entries grey out with the reason.
  • Not in the mock: painting a brush shape (cursor armed on the canvas), the colour-range click, drag handles, an enable/disable eye per row (not in the slice 1a data model), reordering, an Invert result toggle (open question RFC §7.2).
